### 💼 About Me

I work as an IT officer at a hospital in southern Thailand, where I'm the in-house developer behind the organization's software. I take systems from idea to production: design, build, deploy and maintain. I also handle the infrastructure they run on, from server setup to network switch configuration.

Earlier, I interned as a **Software Engineer at Stream IT** and worked with Spring Boot during an internship in Bangkok.

---

### 🔭 Currently Exploring

🤖 **Local, on-premise AI.** I'm experimenting with running language models entirely in-house, so teams get the benefits of AI while their data never leaves the building. The goal is practical, privacy-first AI tools that people can actually use at work. Early days and hands-on, with a hardware upgrade on the way.
